CHAIRMAN’S MESSAGE

Welcome to the official website of Hyderabad SITE Association of Trade & Industry!

It gives me immense pleasure to extend a warm welcome to all our valued members, industrialists, and visitors. I am deeply honored by the trust and confidence reposed in me by electing me as the Chairman of Hyderabad SITE Association of Trade & Industry (HSATI). One of the most active and vibrant industrial associations in Sindh.

Hyderabad holds a unique position as the second-largest industrial zone of Sindh, contributing significantly to the province’s economy and employment. As Chairman, I consider it my foremost responsibility to safeguard the legitimate interests of our industrial community and to work closely with all stakeholders for the sustainable growth of industries in Hyderabad.

Our Association is fully committed to addressing the challenges faced by the business community, including infrastructure development, utilities, law and order, and facilitation of ease of doing business. We aim to strengthen coordination with government departments and industrial agencies to ensure that the concerns of our members are effectively resolved.

I firmly believe that collective efforts, mutual understanding, and continuous dialogue are the keys to progress. I therefore invite all our members to actively participate, share their valuable suggestions, and contribute toward making HSATI a stronger and more effective platform for industrial development.

Together, we will continue our journey toward a more prosperous and industrially progressive Hyderabad.

Zubair Ghangra

Chairman

Welcome to Hyderabad SITE Association of Trade & Industry (HSATI)! It is indeed an honor for me to serve as the Vice Chairman of HSATI, an association that has long been a strong voice for the industrial community of Hyderabad. I extend my heartfelt gratitude to our members for their trust and confidence in the leadership team.

Our focus at HSATI is to create an enabling environment where industries can flourish with better infrastructure, smoother coordination with government departments, and enhanced business facilitation.
